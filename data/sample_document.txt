NovaTech Employee Handbook (Sample Document)

1. Working Hours
Employees work from Monday to Friday.
Standard working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

2. Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 14 days of annual leave per year.

3. Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval.

4. Professional Development
Each employee receives an annual professional development budget of RM1,200.
The budget may be used for technical courses, certifications, and approved conferences.

5. Medical Benefits
Employees receive outpatient medical coverage of up to RM800 per year.

6. Information Security
Employees must not share passwords, API keys, customer records, or confidential company information.

7. Technical Support
Critical production incidents must be reported to the technical lead immediately.
